Mission

The absolute facts about the vehicle and how it arrives. Every field reaches at least one constant the optimiser uses.

Priorities

A 100-point zero-sum allocation over the five shoulds. The budget is a conservation law, not a UI convention: weights are not scale-free here, so buying more of everything would quietly weaken every shall. Points can never reach a barrier.

Compliance

Nine shall rows from the barrier terms and five shoulds from the objective terms — read from wheel_objective rather than retyped, so the taxonomy cannot drift from the one the optimiser enforces. A missed should never makes a design non-compliant.
